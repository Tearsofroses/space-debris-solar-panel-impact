\section{Hypothesis}
\label{sec:hypothesis}

From conservation of linear and angular momentum, we expect that a
fragment of about one gram moving at about $10\unit{km\,s^{-1}}$ will change
the velocity of a 500~kg satellite by only about
$m v_0/M \approx 20\unit{mm\,s^{-1}}$, which is very small (around
$10^{-6}$) compared with the orbital speed. However, if it hits the panel
far from the centre of mass, the satellite should start to spin at a rate
we can notice, around $1^\circ\unit{s^{-1}}$. We also expect that in a
completely inelastic hit almost all of the fragment's kinetic energy
(a fraction close to $M/(m+M)$) is lost as heat and damage.
